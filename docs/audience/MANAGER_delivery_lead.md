# Persona: Delivery Manager

## Overview

> **Purpose:** Characterize the manager accountable for predictable delivery and team health.
> Uses the knowledge base to ground status conversations in systemic trade-offs rather than blunt metrics.

* **Role Focus:** People leadership and delivery assurance across one or two squads.
* **Primary Function:** Align priorities, shield teams from noise, and cultivate learning environments.
* **Environment / Context:** Scale-ups or digital units in larger orgs; mixed maturity in agile/lean practices.

---

## Core Motivations

* **Professional Drivers:** Keep commitments credible, maintain stakeholder trust, and grow adaptable teams.
* **Emotional or Cognitive Drivers:** Calm pragmatist; values transparency, hates surprise escalations.
* **Systemic Positioning:** Serves as the connective layer between product, engineering, and business leadership.

---

## Desiderata

| Category    | Expectation / Need     | Description                                                        |
|-------------|------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Information | Honest flow indicators | Needs nuanced metrics (lead time, quality signals) over vanity KPIs.|
| Interaction | Reciprocal trust       | Expects candid dialogue with tech leads, PMs, and stakeholders.    |
| Support     | Proven playbooks       | Looks for retrospectives, facilitation guides, and scenario checklists.|
| Governance  | Clarity on constraints | Requires visibility into risk/finance limits to negotiate trade-offs.|

---

## Frustrations and Constraints

* **Pain Points:** Competing initiatives without capacity planning; last-minute scope churn.
* **Trade-Off Awareness:** Often arbitrates between product ambition and technical sustainability.
* **Environmental Constraints:** Dependent on other departments for staffing, tooling, and vendor approvals.

---

## Behavioral Cues

| Situation            | Typical Behavior                               | Interpretation                                      |
|----------------------|-------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Stable / Routine     | Hosts lightweight ops reviews, tracks trends   | Prefers steady cadence over heroics.                 |
| Change / Uncertainty | Reframes priorities, aligns stakeholders       | Seeks shared narrative before execution pivots.      |
| Under Pressure       | Triages ruthlessly, communicates impacts early | Optimizes for integrity of commitments.              |

---

## Collaboration Preferences

* **Decision Style:** Context-first; aligns on “why” before “who/when.”
* **Communication Style:** Weekly written briefs + focused syncs; values visual aids (maps, diagrams).
* **Feedback Expectations:** Appreciates concise, specific signals; expects reciprocation when giving tough feedback.

---

## Narrative Summary

A grounded manager who treats delivery as a relationship between people, systems, and expectations. They tap the repository for language that helps non-technical stakeholders understand why certain practices matter, and to coach teams through calm, fact-based retros.
