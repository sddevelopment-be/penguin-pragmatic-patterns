# Persona: Reflective Mentor

## Overview

> **Purpose:** Articulate the veteran practitioner who invests energy in shaping people and culture rather than owning roadmaps.
> They rely on the pattern library to supply language, prompts, and exercises for mentees.

* **Role Focus:** Capability stewardship—coaching engineers, facilitators, and emerging leaders.
* **Primary Function:** Surface blind spots, model reflective practice, and anchor teams in sustainable habits.
* **Environment / Context:** Operates across guilds, communities of practice, and advisory circles inside hybrid or remote organizations.

---

## Core Motivations

* **Professional Drivers:** Help others build judgment faster; ensure lessons survive reorganizations.
* **Emotional or Cognitive Drivers:** Calm, patient presence; believes honesty plus care beats pep-talks.
* **Systemic Positioning:** Acts as an informal safety net—supports teams regardless of reporting lines.

---

## Desiderata

| Category    | Expectation / Need    | Description                                                        |
|-------------|-----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Information | Context synchronicity | Needs transparent goals and constraints to tailor mentoring.       |
| Interaction | Dialogue over monologue| Prefers co-created plans, reflective questions, and office hours.  |
| Support     | Referenceable artifacts| Seeks concise primers, canvases, and exercises to share.           |
| Governance  | Psychological safety   | Requires leadership backing so candid mentoring is welcome.        |

---

## Frustrations and Constraints

* **Pain Points:** Mentoring treated as “spare time” work; mentees seeking answers instead of inquiry.
* **Trade-Off Awareness:** Balances personal billable/roadmap commitments with invisible coaching labor.
* **Environmental Constraints:** Scattered teams, limited shared rituals, inconsistent recognition mechanisms.

---

## Behavioral Cues

| Situation            | Typical Behavior                               | Interpretation                                    |
|----------------------|-------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| Stable / Routine     | Curates reading lists, hosts small circles      | Builds scaffolding for autonomous learning.        |
| Change / Uncertainty | Introduces frameworks, anchors on first principles| Offers sense-making without seizing authority.   |
| Under Pressure       | Slows conversations, reframes stakes            | Protects dignity; avoids panic-fueled decisions.   |

---

## Collaboration Preferences

* **Decision Style:** Co-creative; invites mentees to weigh trade-offs themselves.
* **Communication Style:** Narrative examples, structured debriefs, asynchronous journaling prompts.
* **Feedback Expectations:** Appreciates reciprocal reflection—mentees owning next steps and reporting back.
