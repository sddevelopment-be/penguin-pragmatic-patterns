# Self-Coaching with ChatGPT
*A walkthrough for reflective professionals seeking clarity, alignment, and meaningful growth*

This guide is modeled on the real-life self-coaching process of Stijn Dejongh, and is designed to help you use ChatGPT (or any reflective partner) to gain strategic insight and personal clarity.

---

## Step 1: Set Your Intent

**Goal:** Define why you're doing this. Are you trying to:

- Understand your working style?
- Align your career path with your values?
- Reflect before a transition?
- Explore your leadership identity?

**Suggested prompt:**

> I’d like your help in understanding myself better — not just traits, but patterns, values, and where I’m heading. I’m especially interested in [career development / role design / leadership identity / public voice].

---

## Step 2: Provide Materials

Upload or summarize:
- Personality test results (MBTI, HEXACO, DISC, etc.)
- Résumé, career timeline, or portfolio links
- Past writing or journal entries
- Feedback from peers or clients

**Suggested prompt:**

> Here are some assessment reports, a résumé, and a few things I’ve written. Can you help synthesize patterns, strengths, and growth opportunities?


---

## Step 3: Ask for a Deep Synthesis

Go beyond labels. Ask for patterns, contradictions, and themes.

**Suggested prompt:**

> Based on everything I’ve shared so far, what do you notice about my strengths, gaps, values, and working style? Where do you see recurring themes or contradictions?


---

## Step 4: Actively Interact

Correct, expand, or challenge. You're shaping a narrative, not taking a test.

**Suggested prompts:**

> I see [this pattern] in my work. Can you help me explore it further? What are the implications for my career?

> That part about my communication style doesn’t fully resonate. Let me clarify what I meant when I said... Can you adjust your interpretation?


---

## Step 5: Request Strategic Deliverables

Choose formats that make insights actionable.

- **Executive Profile**

> Can you write an executive summary of me — for stakeholders, hiring managers, or clients?

- **User Manual**

> Can you create a ‘Manual of Me’ — something I could share with a team or coach to explain how I work and what I need?

- **Development Narrative**

> Can you write a reflection-based narrative about how I’ve grown, where I am now, and what I’m aiming for?

- **Competency Framework Review**

> Using a model like the Bredemeyer Architect Competency Framework, can you rate my current level across dimensions, and explain why?

> Can you help me identify the most relevant competencies for my next career step?

- **Next Steps Plan**

> Based on your analysis, what should I focus on next — in terms of skill development, visibility, energy management, or mindset shifts?


---

## Step 6: Personalize It

Bring your personality into the process — your tone, quirks, and principles matter.

**Suggested prompt:**

> I have a dry sense of humor and I show up as a person, even in formal settings. Please incorporate that into how you write this.


---

## Step 7: Request Reflection & Closure

Ask the model to summarize what it’s learned — and what you should take with you.

**Suggested prompt:**

> Please summarize what you learned about me through this process. What should I remember most going forward?


---

## Step 8: Integration

Ask for:
- Markdown export (like this one)
- A clean PDF for journaling or sharing
- Prompts for further writing or coaching
- Conversation summary for personal notes or peer review

---

_This process isn’t about self-improvement — it’s about coherence._  
*Start with who you are. Align with what matters. Build accordingly.*
