# Persona: People Operations Partner

## Overview

> **Purpose:** Represent HR professionals supporting engineering/product groups while advocating humane practices.
> They reference the knowledge base to translate technical culture into people programs.

* **Role Focus:** Talent stewardship—recruiting, performance frameworks, learning pathways.
* **Primary Function:** Align people initiatives with real engineering needs (skills, rituals, incentives).
* **Environment / Context:** Mid-size tech companies balancing growth with culture debt.

---

## Core Motivations

* **Professional Drivers:** Build credible career paths and retention strategies rooted in practice reality.
* **Emotional or Cognitive Drivers:** Empathetic, data-aware; prefers evidence over slogans.
* **Systemic Positioning:** Bridge between leadership, HR policy, and engineering cohorts.

---

## Desiderata

| Category    | Expectation / Need       | Description                                                       |
|-------------|--------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Information | Authentic practice stories| Needs examples to inform competency models and learning plans.    |
| Interaction | Access to tech mentors    | Seeks regular dialogues with leads to calibrate initiatives.      |
| Support     | Playbooks & templates     | Wants ready-to-use facilitation guides, retros, and canvases.     |
| Governance  | Shared accountability     | Requires leadership backing when introducing progressive policies.|

---

## Frustrations and Constraints

* **Pain Points:** Being invited late to change initiatives; lack of visibility into team dynamics.
* **Trade-Off Awareness:** Balances compliance requirements with modern learning/feedback practices.
* **Environmental Constraints:** Small team, many stakeholders; must justify programs with data.

---

## Behavioral Cues

| Situation            | Typical Behavior                                   | Interpretation                                      |
|----------------------|----------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Stable / Routine     | Curates enablement resources, tracks sentiment     | Seeks slow, steady culture improvements.             |
| Change / Uncertainty | Hosts listening sessions, maps impacts             | Needs shared vocabulary to prevent rumor loops.      |
| Under Pressure       | Escalates risks, asks for executive sponsorship    | Protects people systems from reactive policy shifts. |

---

## Collaboration Preferences

* **Decision Style:** Consultative; validates assumptions with cross-disciplinary partners.
* **Communication Style:** Written summaries with data snapshots; follows up with workshops.
* **Feedback Expectations:** Direct, respectful; wants context on constraints and desired outcomes.
