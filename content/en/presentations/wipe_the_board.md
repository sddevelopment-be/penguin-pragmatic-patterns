## "Everything is In Progress"

---

### What happens?

* Paused tasks, forgotten drafts, half-baked ideas
* "Zombie work" stays visible just because it once started
* Bloated boards => false progress and mental clutter

---

## Wipe the Board

<img src="/images/presentations/wip_wipe/wipe_the_board.webp" style="width: 60%;" />

---

### 6 daily steps  

1. Clear the board
2. Re-evaluate each task
3. Be strict: no "maybe" zones
4. Move leftovers to backlog/cold shelf
5. Recommit to a few
6. Leave the rest behind

---

### Intent

* Make WIP a decision, not a default
* Refocus your attention daily
* Reduce inventory waste
* Create emotional clarity by letting go

---

### Why it works

* WIP = Inventory = Liability
* Progress ≠ volume, but flow
* Tasks you can’t finish are bottlenecks

--- 

### This Isn't Easy — And That’s the Point

* Habit-building required — may feel unnatural at first
* Risk of appearing “unproductive” externally
* Doesn't unblock tasks, only makes them visible
* Needs cultural safety to uncommit without shame

---

## Go Forth and Wipe the Board

* Start with an empty "In Progress" column
* Ask **“Would I start this today?”**
* Recommit only to a few tasks
* Shelve the rest (cold shelf, % done, reason)
* Feel lighter. Focus better.
