# Persona: Value Stream Facilitator

## Overview

> **Purpose:** Capture the delivery-focused leader who translates strategy into sustainable flow across teams.
> They lean on pragmatic patterns to keep discussions grounded in evidence rather than theatre.

* **Role Focus:** Cross-team coordination—balancing product intent, capacity, and sociotechnical health.
* **Primary Function:** Remove systemic blockers, orchestrate improvement experiments, and defend reflective time in the roadmap.
* **Environment / Context:** Operates in multi-team product groups (50–150 people) with partial platformization and constant stakeholder scrutiny.

---

## Core Motivations

* **Professional Drivers:** Keep commitments credible, protect delivery predictability, and make improvement work visible.
* **Emotional or Cognitive Drivers:** Prefers calm confidence; values nuance over simplistic velocity metrics.
* **Systemic Positioning:** Acts as connective tissue between product, engineering, and leadership forums—translator and negotiator.

---

## Desiderata

| Category    | Expectation / Need     | Description                                                     |
|-------------|------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|
| Information | Shared vocabulary      | Needs reusable frames (patterns, primers) to guide facilitation.|
| Interaction | Transparent feedback   | Requires candid signals from tech leads and PMs to course-correct. |
| Support     | Lightweight diagnostics | Wants checklists, canvases, and questions that surface bottlenecks.|
| Governance  | Outcome-focused metrics| Prefers measures tied to learning, flow, and trust over vanity KPIs.|

---

## Frustrations and Constraints

* **Pain Points:** Strategy swings that reset priorities; teams overloaded with “just one more” initiative.
* **Trade-Off Awareness:** Constantly negotiates between immediate delivery optics and foundational capability work.
* **Environmental Constraints:** Limited budget for tooling or coaching; must influence without formal authority.

---

## Behavioral Cues

| Situation            | Typical Behavior                                  | Interpretation                                      |
|----------------------|---------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Stable / Routine     | Hosts quiet retros, curates dashboards            | Uses data plus narrative to sustain trust.           |
| Change / Uncertainty | Runs experiments, socializes decision trees       | Seeks shared understanding before committing teams. |
| Under Pressure       | Simplifies scope, protects critical teams         | Shields people from thrash, escalates trade-offs.   |

---

## Collaboration Preferences

* **Decision Style:** Evidence-informed facilitation; prefers framing choices over mandating outcomes.
* **Communication Style:** Brief asynchronous updates plus periodic deep-dive sessions.
* **Feedback Expectations:** Values direct, “no blame” debriefs that expose systemic blind spots.

A facilitator who treats delivery as a socio-technical system. They turn the pattern library into working agreements, measurement rituals, and talking points that keep multidisciplinary teams aligned without burning them out.
