# Persona: Emerging Developer

## Overview

> **Purpose:** Describe early-career engineers (0–2 years) integrating into socio-technical teams.
> They use the knowledge base to accelerate context-building beyond tutorials.

* **Role Focus:** Implementation and learning inside product squads.
* **Primary Function:** Deliver features while absorbing architecture, practices, and collaboration norms.
* **Environment / Context:** Hybrid teams with lightweight process; mentorship available but bandwidth-limited.

---

## Core Motivations

* **Professional Drivers:** Build credibility through reliable delivery; understand why decisions were made.
* **Emotional or Cognitive Drivers:** Curious, cautious; seeks psychological safety to ask “basic” questions.
* **Systemic Positioning:** Dependent contributor; future multiplier if guided well.

---

## Desiderata

| Category    | Expectation / Need        | Description                                                        |
|-------------|---------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Information | Clear, jargon-light primers| Needs concise explanations of concepts, practices, and tools.      |
| Interaction | Structured feedback loops | Weekly check-ins, code review rituals, pairing opportunities.      |
| Support     | Self-serve references     | Cheatsheets, glossaries, and examples mirroring real deliverables. |
| Governance  | Transparent expectations  | Wants criteria for “good” pull requests, testing, documentation.   |

---

## Frustrations and Constraints

* **Pain Points:** Context fragmentation—different seniors offer conflicting guidance.
* **Trade-Off Awareness:** Must balance speed of learning with delivery pressure.
* **Environmental Constraints:** Limited ownership rights; dependent on tooling access and approvals.

---

## Behavioral Cues

| Situation            | Typical Behavior                       | Interpretation                                      |
|----------------------|----------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Stable / Routine     | Follows checklists, documents discoveries | Building muscle memory through repetition.        |
| Change / Uncertainty | Pauses, seeks clarification via chat   | Needs framing before acting; fear of breaking things.|
| Under Pressure       | Tunnels on tasks, forgets to broadcast | Requires reminders to surface blockers early.       |

---

## Collaboration Preferences

* **Decision Style:** Looks for precedent; prefers concrete examples.
* **Communication Style:** Async messages plus occasional pairing; appreciates annotated reviews.
* **Feedback Expectations:** Actionable, kind, and specific; aware of growth areas but needs prioritization.

---

## Measures of Success

| Dimension   | Indicator                                | Type                        |
|-------------|------------------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Performance | Stories delivered with fewer reworks     | Quantitative (rework rate)  |
| Quality     | Tests/docs included without prompting    | Qualitative (review notes)  |

## Narrative Summary

A developer learning how systems and people fit together. They open Pragmatic Penguin when a term shows up in stand-up, when preparing for design reviews, or when trying to justify a testing approach without yet having the vocabulary.
